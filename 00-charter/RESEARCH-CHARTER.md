# RealityDB Research Charter
Version: 1.0
Status: Active

---

# Purpose

This repository exists to discover products through disciplined research rather than invention.

We do not begin with products.

We begin with evidence.

Products emerge only after recurring problems, unmet needs, and commercially viable capabilities have been demonstrated through independent investigation.

---

# Mission

Conduct rigorous, evidence-based product discovery that identifies opportunities worth building.

The objective is not to prove ideas correct.

The objective is to discover whether they deserve to exist.

---

# Research Philosophy

We believe that:

Evidence is superior to opinion.

Repeated observations are stronger than isolated examples.

Contradictions are more valuable than agreement.

Blind spots are opportunities.

A rejected hypothesis is a successful research outcome.

The cost of killing a weak idea early is significantly lower than building the wrong product.

---

# Guiding Principles

## 1. Evidence Before Conclusions

Every significant conclusion must be supported by evidence.

Conclusions without supporting evidence are hypotheses.

---

## 2. Observation Before Interpretation

Separate:

What was observed

from

What it may mean.

Never confuse facts with interpretations.

---

## 3. Preserve Contradictions

Contradictory findings must never be discarded.

Contradictions often reveal:

- hidden customer segments
- market fragmentation
- different buying behaviors
- incorrect assumptions
- emerging opportunities

---

## 4. Preserve Uncertainty

If evidence is insufficient, explicitly state:

"We do not know."

Research exists to reduce uncertainty—not eliminate it.

---

## 5. Product Neutrality

Researchers must never become emotionally attached to a product direction.

The goal is discovery.

Not validation.

---

## 6. Capability Before Product

Research first identifies:

problems

↓

recurring capabilities

↓

commercial capabilities

↓

products

A capability does not automatically justify a product.

---

## 7. Commercial Reality

Technical elegance alone is insufficient.

Every proposed capability must eventually answer:

Who buys this?

Why now?

Why us?

Why not an existing solution?

# What Success Looks Like

Successful research produces:

Validated problems

Evidence-backed capabilities

Commercial insight

Clear customer definition

Known competitors

Known risks

Known assumptions

Known unknowns

Research is not successful because it produces a product.

Research is successful because it improves decision quality.

---

# Investigator Independence

Each investigator performs research independently.

Investigators must not:

Coordinate findings

Copy conclusions

Seek consensus

Optimize for agreement

Independent thinking produces stronger evidence.

---

# Research Director

The Research Director does not perform primary research.

The Research Director:

evaluates

questions

challenges

compares

tests

rejects

prioritizes

The Research Director is responsible for protecting the organization from weak conclusions.

---

# Decision Rule

Agreement does not determine truth.

Popularity does not determine truth.

Confidence does not determine truth.

Evidence determines truth.

---

# Default Assumption

Assume every hypothesis is incomplete until sufficient evidence proves otherwise.

The burden of proof always belongs to the hypothesis.

---

# Standard of Proof

A recommendation should not advance toward product definition unless:

the problem is recurring

the customer is identifiable

the economic value is meaningful

existing alternatives are understood

adoption barriers are known

commercial viability is plausible

critical assumptions have been investigated

major blind spots have been documented

If these conditions are not met:

Research continues.

---

# Repository Principle

Every document inside this repository should answer one question:

"What evidence supports this?"

If that question cannot be answered, the document is incomplete.

---

# Final Principle

The objective of this repository is not to create products.

The objective is to discover products worth creating.
"Fall in love with the problem. Be skeptical of the solution. Let the evidence decide."
