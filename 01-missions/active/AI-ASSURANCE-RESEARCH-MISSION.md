\# Mission Statement



Conduct an evidence-based investigation into how enterprise and regulated organizations evaluate, test, validate, monitor, govern, and assure production AI systems and AI agents.



Determine whether the evidence supports a commercially viable opportunity for RealityDB to expand beyond synthetic data generation into AI assurance infrastructure.



The objective is not to validate an existing product idea.



The objective is to determine whether a defensible business opportunity actually exists.



The investigation must actively attempt to reject weak hypotheses rather than confirm preferred outcomes.



\---



\# Background



RealityDB currently focuses on production-realistic synthetic data.



Core strengths include:



\- production-realistic synthetic datasets

\- domain-specific schemas

\- temporal realism

\- referential integrity

\- deterministic generation

\- simulation environments

\- compliance-oriented deployments

\- production testing infrastructure



Large organizations are rapidly deploying:



\- AI assistants

\- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

\- autonomous agents

\- workflow automation

\- decision-support systems



These systems introduce new operational risks that traditional software testing often fails to address.



Organizations are beginning to invest in:



\- evaluation

\- monitoring

\- governance

\- compliance

\- release approval

\- AI risk management



This mission investigates whether RealityDB possesses capabilities that naturally extend into this emerging market.

\---



\# Source Quality



Apply the source hierarchy defined by the RealityDB Research System.



Prefer:



Tier S sources for binding requirements, official records, and verified primary evidence.



Tier A sources for authoritative technical, commercial, and operational evidence.



Tier B sources for credible supporting evidence.



Use Tier C sources cautiously.



Tier D and Tier E sources may identify leads but cannot independently justify major strategic conclusions.



\---



\# Source Independence



Multiple sources do not automatically represent multiple independent evidence chains.



Treat the following as a single evidence chain when they repeat the same originating claim:



\- syndicated articles

\- vendor press releases republished by media outlets

\- analyst reports derived from the same survey

\- blogs quoting the same customer announcement

\- job-posting aggregators reproducing one original posting

\- multiple summaries of one regulatory document

\- social posts linking to one original source



Investigators must identify the likely original source whenever possible.

Evidence Confidence



Use the following confidence labels:



High



Supported by strong, independent evidence with minimal unresolved contradiction.



Moderate



Supported by meaningful evidence, but material uncertainty remains.



Low



Supported by limited, indirect, weak, or highly conflicting evidence.



Unknown



The available evidence is insufficient to form a defensible conclusion.



Confidence must reflect the evidence, not the investigator's writing confidence.

Evidence Saturation



Investigators should continue researching until one of the following occurs:



additional credible sources repeatedly confirm existing findings

new sources no longer materially change the conclusion

critical evidence cannot be accessed

the research limit is reached

conflicting evidence requires escalation



Do not confuse high source volume with evidence saturation.



Ten derivative articles may add less value than one independent implementation record.

\# Deliverable Requirements



The final research submission must be sufficiently complete that an executive team could make a strategic investment decision without conducting additional background research.



Every conclusion must be traceable to evidence.



Assertions without evidence are not acceptable.



\---



\# Required Deliverables



Every investigator must submit the following sections in the order shown.



\## 1 Executive Summary



Maximum three pages.



Include:



\- mission objective

\- major findings

\- principal risks

\- contradictory evidence

\- confidence assessment

\- strategic recommendation



\---



\## 2 Research Methodology



Describe:



\- search strategy

\- source selection

\- exclusion criteria

\- evidence scoring approach

\- limitations

\- unresolved questions



\---



\## 3 Market Overview



Summarize:



\- market maturity

\- major trends

\- current adoption

\- expected direction

\- notable uncertainties



\---



\## 4 Evidence Catalogue



Present every evidence record using the required evidence template.



Evidence IDs must remain stable throughout the report.



\---



\## 5 Research Findings



Organize findings by research area.



Each finding must include:



Finding ID



Supporting Evidence IDs



Confidence



Commercial relevance



Technical relevance



Contradictory evidence



Open questions



\---



\## 6 Competitor Analysis



Summarize every major competitor using the required framework.



Provide comparative tables whenever appropriate.



\---



\## 7 Buyer Analysis



Summarize:



buyers



users



approvers



procurement stakeholders



success metrics



decision criteria



budget ownership



\---



\## 8 Opportunity Analysis



Summarize:



market attractiveness



buyer urgency



technical feasibility



competitive landscape



RealityDB strategic fit



implementation complexity



commercial risk



\---



\## 9 Contradictions



Present every contradiction record.



Explain:



current evidence



remaining uncertainty



recommended follow-up



\---



\## 10 Blind Spots



Present every blind-spot record.



Rank by severity.



\---



\## 11 Research Debt



Present every unresolved question.



Prioritize by strategic importance.



\---



\## 12 Final Recommendation



Select exactly one recommendation.



Support it using evidence.



State:



confidence



major assumptions



conditions that would reverse the recommendation



\---



\# Report Quality Checklist



Before submission verify that:



Every finding references evidence.



Every evidence record references a source.



Every recommendation references findings.



Every contradiction has been documented.



Every unresolved issue appears in Research Debt.



Every hypothesis has been evaluated.



No unsupported assertions remain.

\# Research Director Review



After all investigator reports have been received, the Research Director will:



verify evidence quality



identify duplicate evidence



identify conflicting findings



evaluate source independence



identify unsupported conclusions



compare investigator recommendations



measure agreement



produce an integrated executive assessment



No investigator report will be accepted without independent critical review.



\---



\# Acceptance Criteria



Mission 001 shall be considered complete only when:



The Research Director certifies that:



\- the investigation satisfies the Research Charter

\- the evidence supports executive decision-making

\- contradictions have been addressed

\- confidence has been assigned appropriately

\- remaining uncertainty has been documented

\- strategic recommendations are evidence-based

\- research debt has been recorded

\- no material gaps remain that would invalidate the recommendation



Only after these conditions are satisfied may Mission 001 be closed and archived.
