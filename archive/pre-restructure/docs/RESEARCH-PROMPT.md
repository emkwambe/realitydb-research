# Deep Research Prompt

Act as a senior market researcher, AI evaluation architect, enterprise software strategist, and product discovery analyst.

Conduct a rigorous, evidence-based investigation into this question:

**How are regulated and enterprise companies building evaluation, testing, monitoring, and assurance infrastructure for production AI agents?**

The purpose is to determine whether RealityDB should extend from synthetic data generation into production-realistic AI evaluation scenarios, benchmark datasets, testing environments, and release-governance infrastructure.

Investigate:

- Healthcare
- Insurance
- Financial services
- Customer support platforms
- Enterprise SaaS
- Legal technology

For each company and job posting, extract:

- Responsibilities
- Data sources
- Metrics
- Tools and technologies
- Team ownership
- Compliance and safety requirements
- Recurring failures
- Internal systems being built

The final analysis must identify recurring capability patterns, market maturity, build-versus-buy behavior, competitive gaps, RealityDB strategic fit, and the strongest narrow product wedge.

Do not assume a standalone product should exist. Let the evidence determine whether the opportunity is a product, feature, consulting service, infrastructure layer, or market to avoid.
