# Research Brief

## Primary research question

How are regulated and enterprise companies building evaluation, testing, monitoring, and assurance infrastructure for production AI agents?

## Strategic purpose

Evaluate whether a defensible opportunity exists for RealityDB to expand into:

- Production-realistic evaluation datasets
- Synthetic agent scenarios
- Benchmark packs
- Failure and edge-case generation
- AI testing environments
- Assurance and release-governance infrastructure

## Required evidence

For each target industry, extract:

- Responsibilities
- Data sources
- Metrics
- Tools
- Team ownership
- Compliance requirements
- Recurring failures
- Internal systems being built

## Target sample

- At least 50 relevant job postings
- At least 8 postings per major industry when available
- Mix of startups, mid-market companies, and enterprises
- Primarily postings from the previous 18 months

## Decision outcomes

The final recommendation must be one of:

- Pursue immediately
- Validate with design partners
- Build only as a RealityDB feature
- Offer first as consulting
- Monitor but do not build
- Reject the opportunity
