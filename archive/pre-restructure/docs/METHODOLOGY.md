# Research Methodology

## 1. Source hierarchy

1. Job postings
2. Company engineering blogs and technical documentation
3. Public repositories and architecture materials
4. Conference talks and technical interviews
5. Regulatory guidance and standards
6. Vendor case studies
7. Secondary market commentary

## 2. Evidence classification

Each finding should be tagged as:

- `verified` — directly stated by a primary source
- `supported_inference` — inferred from multiple credible sources
- `hypothesis` — plausible but not yet sufficiently supported
- `contradicted` — meaningful evidence points in the opposite direction

## 3. Unit of analysis

The primary unit is a company capability, not a job title.

Different job titles may map to the same underlying capability, including:

- AI evaluation engineering
- LLMOps
- AI platform engineering
- ML reliability
- AI quality engineering
- Responsible AI
- AI observability

## 4. Duplicate handling

Syndicated or duplicated job listings should count once.

## 5. Product opportunity test

A capability is product-worthy when it has:

- Repeated demand across companies
- A recognizable buyer
- High operational or regulatory cost
- Significant internal engineering effort
- Incomplete existing tooling
- A narrow initial workflow
- Strong fit with RealityDB assets
